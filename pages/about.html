Our Story

Built Around The Weather.

Stop Spot Chase was created for people who are fascinated by severe weather and want to experience, understand, and document it.

Storm chasing is about much more than driving toward a storm. It is about understanding the atmosphere, watching conditions change, learning from every chase, and knowing when it is time to back away.

Through live coverage, chase reports, weather information, and field observations, Stop Spot Chase aims to bring the experience closer to everyone who shares a passion for weather.

What We Believe

The Chase Has Principles.

The goal isn't simply to get close to a storm. It is to learn, document, and make responsible decisions.

01

Weather First

In The Field

How We Approach A Chase.

Forecast First

Before heading out, understand the forecast, expected hazards, road network, and overall setup.

Stay Aware

Conditions can change quickly. Continuously monitor warnings, radar, observations, and the environment around you.

Know Your Exit

Never focus so much on the storm that you forget the road, traffic, terrain, or your available escape routes.

Respect Warnings

Official warnings are there for a reason. When conditions become unsafe, safety takes priority.

Learn From Every Chase

Every chase provides information that can improve forecasting, positioning, preparation, and decision-making.
