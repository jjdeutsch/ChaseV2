Weather Safety

Respect The Storm.

When A Warning Is Issued

A warning means hazardous weather is occurring or imminent. Always follow the instructions of official emergency management and weather authorities.

  • Know where your safest shelter is.
  • Keep alerts enabled.
  • Never ignore an emergency warning.
  • Avoid unnecessary travel during dangerous conditions.

Official Information

Stop Spot Chase encourages everyone to use official weather information when making safety decisions.
