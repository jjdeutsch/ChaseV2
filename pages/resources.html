Stop Spot Chase

Chase Preparation

Good storm chasing starts long before the storm arrives.

  • Check the latest forecast.
  • Review watches and warnings.
  • Check road and travel conditions.
  • Keep communications available.
  • Have enough fuel and supplies.
  • Know your safe shelter options.

Safety First

Know When To Stop.

Storm chasing should never come before personal safety. Severe thunderstorms, tornadoes, flooding, lightning, and dangerous road conditions can develop quickly. Always take official warnings seriously and follow instructions from the National Weather Service and local emergency management.

Official Warnings Use official alerts when making safety decisions.
Safe Position Never put yourself or others unnecessarily in danger.
Road Safety Dangerous roads can become a greater threat than the storm.
