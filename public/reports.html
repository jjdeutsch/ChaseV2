Storm Reports

Share a severe weather observation with the Stop Spot Chase team.

Submit a Report

Include the location, approximate time, and details. For emergencies, contact 911. For official severe weather reporting, follow your local NWS office guidance.

Reporting Safety

Never put yourself in danger to collect weather information. Do not drive into flooded roads, approach tornadoes, or stop in traffic lanes.

Photos and videos should only be collected from a safe location. Do not delay seeking shelter to submit a report.
